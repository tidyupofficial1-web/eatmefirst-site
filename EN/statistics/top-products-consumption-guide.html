Guide: Stock & Usage

Current stock is grouped by product across locations. Quantities are equivalent units: two whole items and one at 50% count as 2.5 units. The inventory total counts entries, not units. Quantities of different products are not compared by weight or value.

The usage section shows recorded removals and partial use in the last 30 days, using the records available since tracking began. Transfers between locations do not count. Undoing an operation corrects the totals. Removal does not yet distinguish use from disposal. Older operations are not reconstructed; current stock does not indicate which product was purchased most often.
