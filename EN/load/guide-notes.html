📝 Notes for this product

Write anything helpful, such as Picnic supplies or Keep for Mum. A note does not change the quantity left: use Partial in Consumption & Usage for that.

Reserve a product or set a reminder

In Consumption & Usage, open Notes and reminders. Enter who or what the product is for, choose the date and how many days early to show the reminder, then save. The event date leaves the expiry unchanged. If the event is after the product date, the app asks you to acknowledge a warning. That confirmation does not establish that the product is safe. The reservation is shown when you remove stock, and you can still confirm the removal. Reminders appear in the app menu when due. They are not sent as emails or as notifications while the app is closed. For a mistaken edit, check Last 15 operations. A later action on the same product may need to be undone first.
