🔒 Strict dates

Select Strict date for a date that must be respected, such as a use-by date on a food label. A date must be entered to save a product with this option.

Warnings are red from 10 days before the date and remain red after it. Without this option, the date is treated as indicative or estimated, with yellow warnings. Entering days, months or an exact date does not determine which type it is.

The app does not extend dates, delete products or decide whether food is safe to eat. Follow the instructions on the packaging.
