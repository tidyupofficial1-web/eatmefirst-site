🔢 How many packs did you buy?

Enter the number of whole packs, boxes or bundles you bought: 1, 2, 3 and so on. Two pasta packets means 2 packs. One bundle of water means 1 pack; put the number of bottles in Items per pack.

For 2 packs containing 12 bottles each, the app adds 24 items. Use whole numbers here. Partial use, such as half a bottle, is recorded later in Consumption & Usage.
