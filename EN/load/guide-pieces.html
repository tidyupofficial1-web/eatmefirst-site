Items in each pack

Leave 1 for a single or loose item, such as a bottle of oil, a bag of flour or a piece of cheese. Enter 6 for six bottles of water, 18 for a carton of eighteen eggs or 4 for four yoghurt pots. Check any suggested pack size. A label reading 500 g gives a weight. Two packs of twelve bottles add 24 bottles. For another carton of eggs bought later, record a new entry.
