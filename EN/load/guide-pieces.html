📦 Items inside each pack

Enter how many individual items each pack contains. Use 1 for a single bottle, a bag of flour or a piece of cheese. A multipack might contain 6 bottles, 18 eggs or 4 yoghurt pots.

The online lookup may suggest a count if it recognises a multipack format. Always check it: 500 g describes weight, not 500 items.

Two packs of 12 bottles add 24 items. A pack of 18 eggs remains a pack of 18; record another purchase with a new product entry. Do not change the pack size to represent a second purchase.
