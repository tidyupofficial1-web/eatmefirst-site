📍 Locations you choose

This is where the product goes. Your last location may already appear: check it before saving. “Potting soil in the fridge? Absolutely not, Max!” Eva would say.

Type a location such as Cellar freezer or Wine shelf. Saved locations remain available even after their products run out. Choose an existing name when you mean the same place.

Capital letters and extra spaces are recognised, but Wine shelf and Wine cellar remain different names. Changing the location for a new purchase does not rename all your older locations.
