📅 No date, days, months or an exact date

Choose how to enter the product date. The date fields start empty: you choose the value, checking the packaging where available. No date means no calculated date and no date warning.

Days: enter a whole number from 1 to 365, counted from the day you add the product. Months: enter 1 to 60. If that day does not exist in the target month, the last day of that month is used. Exact date: choose a calendar date.

Strict date determines the warning type, independently of how you enter the date. Warnings begin 10 days beforehand: red for strict dates, yellow for indicative dates. Earlier dates have a normal appearance. Past dates keep their type colour with a message showing how many days have passed. An estimate is not a guaranteed storage life.

Inventory sorts past dates first, then the nearest dates, then products with no date. Strict dates come first when dates are equal. The Menu opens a list of dates to check.

The count updates when the page opens, when you return and at the change of day while it remains open. No notification is sent with the app closed. Dates never delete products or automatically add them to the shopping list. A recorded postponement is marked ↻; the app never postpones a date on its own.
