📅 No date, days, months or an exact date

Choose how to enter the product date. The date fields start empty: you choose the value, checking the packaging where available. No date means no calculated date and no date warning.

Days: enter a whole number from 1 to 365, counted from the day you add the product. Months: enter 1 to 60. If that day does not exist in the target month, the last day of that month is used. Exact date: choose a calendar date.

Strict date determines the warning type, independently of how you enter the date. Warnings begin 10 days beforehand: red for strict dates and yellow for indicative dates copied directly from the label. An indicative date first estimated in days or months is yellow above 10 days and red at 10 days or fewer. Use Edit in Inventory to enter the label date and update the colour. Past dates keep their type colour with a message showing how many days have passed. An estimate is not a guaranteed storage life.

Inventory sorts past dates first, then the nearest dates, then products with no date. Strict dates come first when dates are equal. The Menu opens a list of dates to check.

The count updates when the page opens, when you return and at the change of day while it remains open. No notification is sent with the app closed. Dates never delete products or automatically add them to the shopping list. A recorded postponement is marked ↻; the app never postpones a date on its own.

How dates and alerts work

Leave the date blank for no alert, choose a calendar date, or enter 1–365 days or 1–60 months from the date you add the item. If the target month has no matching day, the last day of that month is used. The strict-date checkbox sets the type. From ten days out, a suggested date copied from the label is yellow and a strict date is red. An estimate entered in days or months turns red at ten days but remains an estimate. Edit it to use the label date. The menu refreshes when opened, when you return, or when the day changes with it open. There are no alerts while the app is closed. Passing a date does not delete an item or add it to the shopping list.
