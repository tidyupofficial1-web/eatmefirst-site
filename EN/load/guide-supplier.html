🛒 Where did you buy it?

You can enter the supermarket, market or local shop where you bought the product. This is optional. Max says: “Now I’ll remember where I found that cheese Eva loves!”

The last store used is suggested for your next entry: change it if you shopped somewhere else. The store is also kept with the product. There is no complete supplier list suggested by typing the first letters.

Statistics groups products still in stock by their recorded store and lets you filter by store. The count refers to inventory entries, not receipts, shopping trips or money spent.
