🏷️ Categories and subcategories

Categories help you find things quickly, whether you are checking your food or looking for gardening supplies. Choose Food, Drinks, Household cleaning, Personal care, Pets, Gardening or Household supplies. You can also type a new category.

Choose the category first: its subcategories will then be suggested. For example, Pets → Food or Gardening → Potting soil. You can type a new subcategory or leave it empty.

New names are remembered after you confirm the product, even when it runs out. For the same barcode, your last saved classification is suggested again. You can correct it before saving.

Existing products keep their names and categories, even if they were entered in another language. They are not automatically renamed or reclassified. Category means what the product is; location means where you keep it.
