Product name and items without a barcode

If the barcode lookup finds a name, check the suggestion before saving. For a damaged or unknown barcode, loose produce, bread or cheese from the market, or a homemade meal to refrigerate or freeze, type a name such as “Yellow onions” or “Shortbread, 500 g” and choose a category. You can save the item without a picture.

Confirmed data stays in the browser on the computer or phone you use. Scanning the barcode again can show local data and look for updated information online. Typing the first letters of a name does not bring back an old product. After a Fresh Start, you can choose a remembered item from the catalogue even without a barcode. Check its details and enter the new quantity and expiry date.
