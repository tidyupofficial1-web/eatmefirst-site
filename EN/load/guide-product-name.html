✍️ Product name and products without a barcode

If the barcode is recognised and a name is available, it is suggested here. Check it before saving. For market vegetables, bread, sliced cheese, homemade meals or a damaged barcode, simply type a useful name and choose a category. A photo is optional.

Confirmed details stay in the browser on the device you use. Scanning the same barcode can restore local details and look for updated information. Typing the first letters of a name does not retrieve an old product.

After Fresh start, you can choose a remembered product from the catalogue, including products without a barcode. Check its details, enter the new packs purchased and choose a new date. Old quantities, dates and reminders are not reused.
