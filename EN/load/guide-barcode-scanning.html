📷 Barcode scanning and your data

Tap Scan barcode and point your phone at the numeric barcode on the pack. Allow camera access when your browser asks. On a PC, type or paste the number, keeping leading zeros, or use a barcode reader and press Find product. Looking up a barcode does not save a product.

The online lookup asks: “Which product matches this barcode?” It does not send your inventory, quantities or shopping and usage history. Product photos are loaded from external catalogues.

Open Facts searches food, pet food, cosmetics and other products. We request an English name and ingredients where available, with the original text as a fallback. The available fields may include a photo, name, category and multipack size. Check them before saving. A weight such as 500 g is not a count of 500 items.

If a barcode is not found, enter the product yourself. A slow service or a temporary lookup limit produces a different message: it does not prove that the product is unknown. Scanning again tries the search again and starts with your local information. Your saved name corrections and the fields you edit while waiting are respected. A newly found photo can fill a missing photo on matching saved products without changing their quantities or dates.

Your confirmed changes are stored in the browser on both PC and phone. For a separate backup, use Utilities → Export inventory, then copy the file to a USB drive or attach it to an email yourself. No email is sent automatically. A backup restores the situation in that saved copy.
