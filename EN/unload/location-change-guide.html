Move items to another location

Use Move items when a row contains several items. Enter how many to move and choose a remembered location, or write a new one. Read the confirmation before saving.

Moving 2 bottles from a pack of 12 leaves 10 in the original location and 2 in the new one. The original pack size stays recorded.

For a single item, use Partial use, keep its remaining percentage and choose its new location. This also works for an already opened item.

Moves do not count as consumption. Notes, reservations and photos remain attached. You can undo an available move from Last 15 operations.

Confirmed changes are stored in this browser. Your PC and phone do not synchronise automatically: transfer an exported inventory file when changing device. Import replaces the whole situation; it does not merge two independently changed inventories.
