Partial use: show what remains

Eva says: “Half a bottle left? Tell the app half a bottle, and leave the guessing to Max!” Open Partial use and set the slider to the percentage remaining, not the percentage consumed.

For a multipack, the slider applies to ONE item. With 12 bottles, using half of one leaves 11 whole bottles and one half bottle. You can choose where the opened item goes.

At 0%, that item is removed. When the original stock is used up, the app adds its purchase pack to the shopping list. A moved item keeps its link to the original pack.

Read the summary before confirming. Cancel leaves the inventory unchanged. Last 15 operations lets you undo available changes, including related shopping entries. Later changes to the same product may need undoing first.

Notes and reminders lets you reserve stock for a person or event, with an optional event date and reminder days. A warning appears if the product date is earlier. Acknowledging it does not change the product date or certify that food is safe.
