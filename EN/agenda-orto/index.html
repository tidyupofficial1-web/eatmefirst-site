Max’s Garden

I’m Max. OrtoFirst is my garden notebook, from getting home with the shopping to bringing in the harvest.

Under Add stock I log seeds, potting mix and tools. If a product has a barcode, I scan it. I add where I put it away and, if needed, an expiry date or a note.

Under Use stock I record what I use, even half a packet, or move a pack to a new spot. Before buying more, I checkInventory and add what I need to my Shopping list. In thePlanner I keep track of jobs and notes for next season.
