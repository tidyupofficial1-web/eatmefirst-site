Terms of use for EatMeFirst and OrtoFirst

1. Purpose and acceptance

EatMeFirst helps you manage your pantry, food dates and shopping. OrtoFirst helps you organise garden supplies, purchases, work and the garden planner. Tick the acceptance box before entering. You can read these terms again at any time.

2. Free local saving and optional exports

Both apps save confirmed changes automatically and immediately in the browser on your phone or computer. Local saving and core features always remain free, even if you do not export or choose not to subscribe if exports are offered by subscription in the future. Any future price and terms will be shown before subscribing. There are currently no purchases, subscriptions, licence codes or renewals.

3. Keeping and restoring your data

The apps keep their records separately in your browser. They do not synchronise devices or store a server copy of your records. Clearing site data, using private browsing, removing the browser or losing or changing devices may make records unavailable. Export inventory currently covers EatMeFirst only, including inventory, dates and any lists and notes covered by the export. Store the file separately and export again after changes. Import replaces the corresponding current records with the snapshot in the file instead of merging them. OrtoFirst stock, shopping lists, planner and archive are outside that export.

4. Barcodes, external services and privacy

Scanning is intended primarily for phones and needs a compatible camera and browser permission. You can also enter a barcode or type the product manually. An online lookup sends the barcode to the Open Facts network, which can consult the food, pet food, beauty and non-food product catalogues. Catalogue images may come from external sources. The lookup does not send your inventory, quantities, notes or planner records. Check suggested data against the product label. Opening counts for some EatMeFirst screens are explained in the linked privacy notice.

Privacy notice on opening counts

5. Food dates and garden products

EatMeFirst helps you organise dates and check food, but cannot tell whether it is safe to eat. Follow mandatory dates on the label and mark them accordingly. Suggested dates and estimates are reminders. OrtoFirst quantities, remaining percentages and dates are organisational notes. For storage, use and disposal, follow the label and manufacturer’s instructions. For plant protection products, follow applicable requirements, including any interval before harvesting.
