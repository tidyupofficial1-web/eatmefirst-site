Notes and photos to think about later

Seen a gift, a dress or something in a shop window? Save a note, a photo, or both. Nothing is sent to family members automatically. Download image saves a copy; then you decide how to share it.

Type your note, take a photo with Open camera, or choose an image from your device. Camera access needs your permission and a browser that supports it. Wait for the preview, then press Save to noticeboard.

New photos are reduced before saving, to limit browser space. This may reduce detail. Keep the original elsewhere if it matters. A large image may still need cropping.

The cross deletes a note. Last 15 operations can normally restore it. If browser space is insufficient, a separate permanent-delete option may appear: its confirmation explains that the note and photo cannot be recovered from history.

Notes and photos are local to this browser. Export inventory includes them. A copy stored only on the same device does not protect against device failure. Keep an exported copy elsewhere when you need a backup.

Save a note and keep a copy of a photo

Photograph a handwritten shopping list, a price tag or a possible gift, or write down a recipe idea. Use the camera or choose an existing image. Viewing a preview does not save a note. Press Save to noticeboard with text, a photo or both. Use Download image to save a separate copy you can share. The board copy may be compressed and the original in your gallery stays untouched. Export the complete pantry to move the board and its photos to another device. For offline use, the app page also has to be available on that device.
