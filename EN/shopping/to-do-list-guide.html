Remember your errands

Use this list for errands such as collecting medicine, getting spare keys or visiting the hardware shop. Type a reminder and press Add.

Tick an errand to mark it as done. Untick it to make it active again. The cross deletes it. To-do entries do not change product stock or add products to the shopping list.

Changes are saved in this browser. Available changes can be undone from Last 15 operations. Export inventory includes your errands so you can transfer them with the other data.
