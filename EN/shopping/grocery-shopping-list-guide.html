Your shopping list

Red entries are products used up through Consumption & Usage. Green entries are suggestions for products you still have but which might not last until the next shopping cycle. Grey entries are your own notes or new products.

Write quantities and brands directly in the text, for example 1 jar of Brand X jam. Tick an entry to darken it, cross it out and move it below unticked entries. Untick it to bring it back. The cross removes the entry.

Green estimates need at least two months of recorded purchases and use, and enough regular shopping and consumption records. They are suggestions, not instructions to buy. The remaining quantity is shown. You may want to use up stock before a holiday!

If you tick a green suggestion and the stock later runs out, the entry becomes red and unticked. Ticking an item does not add it to inventory: record your purchase in Product Entry.

Last 15 operations can undo available additions, ticks and deletions. Later changes to the same entry may need undoing first. Your phone and PC do not synchronise automatically: transfer your inventory through Utilities. Import replaces the complete situation; it does not merge two copies.
