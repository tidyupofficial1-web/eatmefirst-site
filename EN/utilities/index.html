💬 Advice and support on our website

💾 Export and import inventory

Confirmed changes are saved automatically on your device, always free of charge.

Export inventory creates a file you can open on another device with Import inventory. Exporting an external copy is optional.

Transfer is manual: devices do not synchronise automatically.

Recognise your backup copies

Choose a shared place for the current copy. Check the import preview before replacing your inventory. After exporting, check the download and put the new file in the agreed place.

Guide to transfers and copies

Max, do I have to save every time? When you confirm a product entry, removal or other operation, EatMeFirst stores the change in the browser you are using. This applies to both PC and phone. Where a Save or Confirm button appears, press it to complete the operation: typing in the fields is not enough. You do not then need Export inventory to keep that change on the same device.

So what is the USB drive for? “A spare copy, Max!” Eva would say. If your phone or PC breaks, is lost, or you clear browser data, the saved data there can be lost. With Export inventory you prepare a file to keep elsewhere, such as a USB drive or another device.

Can I email it to myself? Yes: export the file, open your email, attach it and send it yourself. EatMeFirst does not email your inventory automatically. To recover it, download the attachment and choose Import inventory. You recover the situation at the time you exported the file. To include later changes, export a new copy.

Use your phone for product entry and transfer the file to your PC, or use a reader on the PC and take a copy on your phone for shopping. Transfer replaces the complete situation: it does not merge two inventories.

A backup is like a spare key: if you leave it in the same bag as your main keys and lose the bag, it won’t help! Keep the file outside the device and update it after important changes. It helps both with recovery after a fault or data loss and with moving between PC and phone.

Export a file to your device, then copy it to a USB drive or attach it to an email yourself. On the receiving device, choose Import inventory and select that file. No automatic sending and no inventory database on a server.

Includes products, remembered catalogue, lists, notes and reminders, preferences and the last 15 operations. Note photos are included; catalogue images remain links to their sources.

💾 Backups, USB Drive & Leo’s Workshop

1. 🖥️ PC at home, 📱 Phone on the go

The phone is handy for scanning; the PC gives you a bigger view. You choose! When switching, export from the updated device and import that file on the other one. Before leaving, check you have the right shopping list: Eva accepts no excuses in front of the shelves!

2. 💾 USB backup from PC or transfer by email

Confirmed changes are stored in the browser you are using. On a PC or laptop, use Export inventory and save or copy the file to a USB drive. Plugging in a drive does not start an automatic backup. On a phone, export the file and manually attach it to an email.

An external copy helps in three ways:

  • Move your data: transfer your inventory between PC and phone.
  • Backup: if you lose data on the device, recover what is in the file kept elsewhere.
  • Shopping on your phone: transfer an updated copy before leaving. Later changes on the other device do not arrive automatically.

🔄 Updating EatMeFirst

New features and fixes come from the website: connect to the internet and reopen the app. If you cannot see the changes, reload the page. You do not need to download a program package.

⚠️ Advanced reset options

1. Fresh start (Clear quantities)

Clears stock and product dates, keeping barcodes, names, categories, photos, pack sizes and stores in a catalogue you can select in Product Entry. Old product notes and reminders are not reused. Lists, notes on the noticeboard and preferences stay; history and usage estimates restart from zero.

2. Start from scratch

Clears inventory, catalogue, lists, notes, preferences. Keeps your original acceptance of the terms, so you can skip the introduction.
