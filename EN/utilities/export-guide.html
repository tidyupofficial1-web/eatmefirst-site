Switching devices and shopping on your phone

Before changing from PC to phone, export from the device with the complete, updated situation. Transfer that file and import it on the receiving device. Check the preview before confirming.

Take an updated copy on your phone before shopping. Ticks and other changes made there are saved in that browser. To bring them back to the PC, export from the phone and import on the PC before making further changes there.

Two inventories changed independently do not merge. Importing one replaces changes that exist only on the other. Warnings help you choose which to keep; the last-modified date is useful but is not a guarantee of completeness.

You choose how to move the file: USB, a cable, or an email attachment you send yourself. Export does not send an email or synchronise devices automatically.

Keep a separate backup before major changes. Recovery keeps only one earlier local situation; a later transfer or reset replaces that recovery copy.
