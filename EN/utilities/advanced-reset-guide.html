Fresh start or start from scratch

Read the reset summary carefully. Neither reset runs until you tick the acknowledgement and confirm. Cancel keeps the situation as it is.

Fresh start clears active stock, product dates and old product reminders. It keeps product codes, names, photos, categories, pack sizes and stores in a catalogue selectable in Product Entry. Enter quantities and dates for each new purchase. Lists, noticeboard notes and preferences stay; history and consumption estimates restart.

Start from scratch clears inventory, catalogue, lists, notes, history and preferences. Original terms acceptance and service entitlement, including extensions, remain. It does not restart the free period or require the introduction again.

A local recovery copy is kept before either reset, replacing any previous recovery copy. Recover opens its preview. Export a file elsewhere too: a copy in the same browser cannot protect against device failure.

Reset does not change the program files or delete a photo from your phone gallery. The confirmation lists which app data will be cleared.
