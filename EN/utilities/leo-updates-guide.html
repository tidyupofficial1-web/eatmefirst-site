Updating EatMeFirst

New features and fixes come from the website. Connect to the internet and reopen the app. If changes are missing, reload the page. You do not need to download a program package.

Updating the pages and saving your inventory are different things. Your inventory stays in the browser. Export inventory creates a separate file for backup or transfer; it does not update the app.

Do not clear browser data to get an update: that can remove your local inventory. Keep an exported copy outside the device before changing browsers or devices.

During development, pages opened from local files show the version on the computer. The online website changes only after an update is published.
