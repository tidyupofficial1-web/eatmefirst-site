Inventory and product dates

See what is at home without changing it. Search by name, location, category or subcategory. Personal names and categories stay as you entered them, even when you change language.

Combine filters to narrow the list. For example, select Fridge as the location and Food as the category, then choose a subcategory if available. Overdue dates appear first, followed by the nearest upcoming dates, with undated items last.

Quantities show individual items and the percentage remaining. A pack of 12 bottles with 2 moved elsewhere shows 10 in the original location and 2 in the new one. An opened bottle at 50% is displayed separately from a whole one.

Warnings begin 10 days beforehand: red for strict dates and yellow for indicative dates copied directly from a label. An indicative date first estimated in days or months is yellow above 10 days and red at 10 days or fewer. It remains indicative. Use Edit to enter the label date and the colour adjusts to the new date. A passed date keeps its colour and a clear warning. The app does not decide whether food is safe.

The product details are read-only. Tap a product to read its photo, barcode, date, notes, reservation and available catalogue information. Missing allergen information does not mean that allergens are absent. Check the packaging. Online information can be incomplete or in its original language.

Use Consumption & Usage to record consumption, move items or save notes and reminders. Event dates do not replace product dates. A reservation warning does not certify that a product is suitable for the event.

Confirmed data stays in the browser on this device. Your phone and PC do not synchronise automatically. Export inventory in Utilities to keep a separate copy or transfer it. Import replaces the whole saved situation, including lists; it does not merge independent changes.

Date counts refresh when you open or return to the page and when the day changes while it stays open. No notification is sent while the app is closed.
