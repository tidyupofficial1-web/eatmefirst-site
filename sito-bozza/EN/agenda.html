Eva’s household agenda

Hi, I’m Eva. On Friday Gianni has judo and Maria has dance. On Saturday I have a hair appointment and friends are coming for a snack in the afternoon. The agenda helps me see what’s coming up and note what to get ready.

Open my agenda Try with Eva

Add an appointment

Open the household agenda from the app menu. Enter a title and date. You can add a time or note, or repeat the appointment weekly. When you save it, the agenda stores it immediately on your device, free of charge.

The dot in the app menu

If an appointment is due in the next 12 hours, a dot and the words “Appointment coming up” appear on the agenda tile. Appointments without a time are flagged on the day itself.

Past appointments

One-off appointments stay in the past list for seven days. After that, they are removed the next time you open the app menu or agenda. For weekly appointments you’ll see the next date.

Practise with me

Change the hair appointment, add a note for the snack or create an appointment. You can clear the demo or reload the examples. Demo changes stay separate from your own agenda.
