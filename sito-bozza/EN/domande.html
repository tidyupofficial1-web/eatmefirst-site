EATMEFIRST / Questions and contact

Clear answers before you start.

Do I need to register?

No. You don’t need an account or password to use your inventory. If you choose to write to us, you naturally share the email address you send from.

Where are changes saved?

On the phone or PC you’re using, in storage managed by the program you open EatMeFirst with, such as Brave, Chrome or Safari. That program is called a ‘browser’: it isn’t the storage itself, but it keeps the app’s data there. When you confirm an operation and saving succeeds, the change is remembered immediately on your device. Your inventory is not sent to us. Be careful: clearing that program’s data can also delete your inventory; in private windows, data may disappear when you close them. It won’t appear on another device automatically: transfer a copy using Export inventory and Import inventory.

What is an external copy for?

Think of an ATM receipt showing your balance and recent transactions: it describes the situation when you print it. If you make more transactions afterwards, the old receipt doesn’t update itself. An EatMeFirst copy works similarly, but contains data to rebuild your inventory: products, quantities, dates and notes, not just a list to read. If your phone breaks, open the app on another device and use Import inventory to load that copy. If it’s a few days old, you recover the situation from that day; later changes won’t be there and need to be entered again. Better that than losing everything! Remember to keep the file outside the device you work on too: on a PC or USB drive, for example, or email it to yourself and check that you can retrieve it from another device. A receipt left on a broken phone won’t help: that’s why it’s useful to export updated copies and keep them elsewhere.

Which functions are free?

Automatic, immediate saving on your phone will always remain free.

This service is currently free.

Does online lookup read my inventory?

No. To look up a product, EatMeFirst sends external catalogues its barcode number, often the 13 digits printed under the bars. It’s like asking, ‘Which product does this number belong to?’ The catalogue searches for the code and, if found, returns the available information for free: for example, a photo, name, category, ingredients, reported allergens and calories. It may not have everything, or even the product. For this lookup, the app does not send your inventory list, quantities, usage or notes. The code alone doesn’t even say whether you’ve bought the product. The catalogues consulted are Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts, Open Pet Food Facts and Open Products Facts; images also load from their sources. As with any connection to an external website, the service also receives technical data needed for the connection. Always check the packaging, especially for allergens.

Can I use it without Internet?

Features already available on your device work with local data. Looking up new products and loading pages or images may require Internet. We don’t promise that everything is available offline.

Can I manage products without barcodes too?

Yes. Enter the name manually, choose a category and location, and enter the quantity. For products with no expiry date, you can leave date management off.

Did you know we can’t contact you?

Leo here! The app doesn’t ask you to register or give us your email. It’s a bit like being on an island: we don’t have your contact details or know where to reach you. To start a conversation, you take the first step: write to us!

But let’s be clear: that doesn’t mean we’ll start sending you advertising or messages. We’ll use your address only to answer your request and any follow-up questions on the same subject. If we don’t hear from you again, we won’t bother you.

Need an explanation, run into a problem or have an improvement in mind? We’ll do our best to help and listen to your ideas. We can’t promise instant replies, but we’ll read carefully.

Getting in touch is simple: your email so we can reply, and a space for your message. Your name is optional. Quick, with no registration!
