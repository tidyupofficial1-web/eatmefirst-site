INVENTORY · WITH MAX

Let’s open the cupboards… without leaving the chair!

Max

‘Thanks, Leo! Now we know how to add products and record what we use, let’s see what’s still at home. This is my part: Inventory. A list to consult without rummaging through every cupboard.’

Here you look. You don’t change products.

Search, filter and open product details. This page does not change quantities or delete products: use Consumption to record usage or moves, and Add products for a new purchase.

‘So you can look around peacefully: opening the milk’s details doesn’t mean you’ve drunk it!’

What does the list show?

Each product shows its name, picture when available, location and category. Beside it are the available items, any remaining partial content and the date you entered.

  • Ten bottles in the pantry

    See the items you still have, even if the original pack contained twelve.

  • A milk carton at 50%

    The carton is still there, and you can see straight away that about half remains.

  • The same product in two places

    It may have separate rows: some in the pantry and some in the fridge. A partly used item can have its own row too.

Lots of products? Narrow the search.

Type part of a name or choose a location, such as ‘fridge’ only. You can also use category and subcategory, when available.

Combine filters: ‘fridge’ and ‘Drinks’, for example. To see everything again, clear the search and set the choices back to ‘All’.

‘If Eva asks whether there’s any milk left, this time I might answer before she finds it herself!’

First, the products that need checking.

The list is ordered by date, with approaching and past dates first. Alerts distinguish indicative dates in yellow from strict dates in red; products further from the alert threshold look more discreet. The text tells you how long until the date or how long ago it passed.

These are the dates recorded in the app: always check the label and storage conditions too.

Tap a product to open its details.

Read the quantity, remaining content, location, category, date, barcode and your notes or reminders at your own pace. For example: ‘Reserved for Sunday’.

If the consulted catalogues provide more information, you may also find brand, ingredients, energy and reported allergens, with their data source. Not every product has every detail, and some information may be in the catalogue’s original language.

Missing allergen information does not mean the product contains no allergens: check the packaging label.

When you’ve finished, press Close to return to the list.

Max

‘One thing to remember: Inventory shows what we recorded. If we finish the milk without telling the app, it will still think it’s there. That’s why Eva’s little sheet of notes, entered during a break, is such a good idea!’
