Before we go in, let’s read the terms too.

“I know: it’s tempting to go straight to the buttons! But it’s worth pausing here. The terms explain what the app offers, where your data stays, how copies work and the limits of product information.”

Reading them here on the website does not mean accepting them. To use EatMeFirst, enter the application, read the terms and tick the acceptance box there before proceeding.

Your choice is remembered in your device’s browser. Next time, using the same browser and while acceptance remains valid and stored, you go straight to the Menu, where you choose all the features. If you clear browser data, change browser or device, or the terms requiring acceptance are updated, you may be asked to complete this step again.

Read the app’s terms · Still have a question?

A word to remember: browser.

It’s the program you use to open the app, such as Brave, Chrome or Safari. When we say ‘saved in the browser’, the data stays in storage on your phone or PC, managed by that program. We aren’t keeping it on one of our computers.

Want more room on the screen?

Some pages open with an explanation from Max, Eva or Leo. When you’ve finished reading it, you can close it to leave more room for products and buttons.

Where you see Close, you can hide it for now. Where Do not show again or its checkbox is available, you can ask the app to remember your choice for that explanation. On some pages the X is already remembered: follow the wording of the command shown. Info guides remain available.
