STATISTICS · WITH MAX AND LEO

A look at the numbers, without doing the sums yourself.

Max

“Here we can get a clearer picture: what stock we have, which shops supplied the products still at home, and what usage we’ve recorded. Leo makes sure I don’t start inventing numbers, though!”

Three tabs, three ways to look at things.

  • Stores and suppliers

    Find the shops listed on products still in your inventory. Select a store to narrow down what you see.

  • Stock and usage

    See the available quantities for each product, the largest stocks and usage recorded over the last thirty days.

  • Food waste index

    For now, it is shown as not yet calculable: products used need to be distinguished from products thrown away. A product whose date has passed stays visible in Inventory with a warning; it is not currently deleted automatically. The app doesn’t know whether you used it or threw it away. When you record in Consumption that it has run out, it is added to the shopping list. To calculate waste, we will also need to distinguish the reason for removal.

Leo

“Exactly, Max. Better to say clearly what we know than show a lovely percentage that means nothing!”

Half a product counts as half.

A full bottle counts as one unit; a bottle at 50% counts as half an equivalent unit. This includes remaining contents in the totals for stock and recorded movements.

It doesn’t mean comparing the weight of different products: an egg and a bottle are still two different things.

Usage depends on what you record.

The figures use removals and partial usage recorded during the available period. Moving a bottle from the cupboard to the fridge is not usage. Undoing a movement corrects the related totals.

Movements from before data collection began are not reconstructed. Consumption does not yet distinguish use from disposal: that is another reason we don’t present a food waste index as if it were already measurable.

Eva

“So if I forget to record the eggs for my cake, it’s not Leo’s sums that are wrong! All right, I get it: first I update my notes, then I look at the numbers.”

These numbers stay on your device too.

Your phone or PC reads the inventory data, does the calculations and shows the results on your screen. For these statistics, we receive nothing: no product list, no usage records and no calculation results.

Each tab also has its own guide: open it, read the explanation and return to Statistics.
