UTILITIES · WITH MAX

Your inventory, on another device too.

Max

“Eva has prepared your shopping list, Leo has explained usage… now I’m back. Utilities is our little toolbox. Not the drawer where we throw everything and then can’t find anything, mind you!”

Your phone saves changes automatically.

Automatic, immediate saving on your phone will always remain free.

We thought it could be useful to have your inventory on another device too. Export inventory creates a file with your products, quantities, dates, notes and lists. Use Import inventory to open it on the device you choose.

This service is currently free.

Max

You can start on your phone and open the saved inventory on another device. Handy, isn’t it?

A handy file to take with you.

Choose where to keep the file: a folder you can easily recognise, another device, a USB drive or your email. Keep the updated copy in the same place so you know where to find it.

You transfer the file yourself. EatMeFirst does not send it automatically or synchronise devices. After exporting, check that the file is in your chosen location.

Import: reopen the inventory you saved.

Here’s an example: you made a copy of your inventory on your phone and transferred the file to your PC. To find your phone’s products and notes on your PC, open EatMeFirst on the PC, go to Utilities and press Import inventory. Choose the file you transferred. The same procedure lets you recover a copy after changing devices.

Before proceeding, the app shows two sets of numbers: how many products and notes you have now, and how many the file contains. Nothing has changed yet. To use the file’s data, press Confirm replacement; otherwise choose Keep current situation.

Transferring replaces the complete situation; it does not merge two inventories. If you work on your PC and then your phone, first transfer the updated copy to the other device. Making separate changes on both can leave you with two different situations.

Before replacement, a recoverable local copy of the previous situation is kept. It helps you go back, but stays in the same browser: it doesn’t replace a copy kept elsewhere.

Want to start again? Choose carefully.

Fresh start clears your active stock and its dates, keeping remembered products in the catalogue so you don’t have to enter them again. Lists and notes stay; operation history and usage calculations start again.

Start from scratch also clears the catalogue, lists, notes and preferences. It does not restart the free period for the external copy service. Before using this command, create a copy with Export inventory and keep it elsewhere. Pressing Start from scratch opens a window explaining which data will be deleted and which will stay. That’s what we mean by a ‘summary’: an explanation to read before giving your final confirmation.

Notes, tasks and recent operations.

In the shopping section you’ll also find To-do list and Notepad: jot down an errand, keep a note or add a photo. For example, a gift spotted in a shop window that you want to show someone at home.

Photos saved in sticky notes are automatically included in the file created with Export inventory: you don’t need to select them individually. Product images retrieved from online catalogues remain links to their sources.

When you no longer need a photo, delete its sticky note with the X. If you want to keep the image, download it first. Removing unnecessary photos helps leave room for the app’s other data. To fully recover that space, old recovery copies and undoable operations containing the photo may also need to be replaced. A photo already included in an older exported file stays in that file.

With Last 15 operations you can find recent movements and undo those that are available. The app tells you if you need to undo a later step on the same product first.

“And if a command isn’t clear, open its info point. The tools are there: you don’t have to guess how they work!”

A word to remember: browser.

It’s the program you use to open the app, such as Brave, Chrome or Safari. When we say ‘saved in the browser’, we mean the data stays in storage on your phone or PC, managed by that program. We aren’t keeping it on one of our computers.
