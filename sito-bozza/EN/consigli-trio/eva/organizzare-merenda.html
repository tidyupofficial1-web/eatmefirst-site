How to plan an afternoon snack for children and their friends

Count the guests, ask families about dietary needs and check what’s at home before shopping.

Start with the guests, then plan the food

Count how many children are coming. Ask their families directly about allergies or other dietary needs. Then choose what to serve and check what you already have. Look at the packaging when you need to check ingredients.

Make a list of only what’s missing

If you already have biscuits and napkins, leave them off the list. Check cups and drinks too. EatMeFirst lets you put food and supplies such as napkins and cups on the same shopping list. The inventory, list and automatic browser saving are free, with no installation or sign-up.
