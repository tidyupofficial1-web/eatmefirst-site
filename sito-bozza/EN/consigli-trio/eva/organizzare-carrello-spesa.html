How to pack a grocery cart

A simple order for protecting eggs, bread and other delicate groceries.

Heavy items below, delicate items on top

Start with bottles, cans and sturdy packages at the bottom. Leave room for eggs, bread and ripe fruit so they stay clear of heavier items.

Pick up cold foods last

Get chilled and frozen food near the end. Keep packaged raw meat or fish separate from other foods and use an insulated bag on the way home, especially in warm weather.
