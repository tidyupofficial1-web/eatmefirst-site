How to make a school supplies list without buying duplicates

Check pencil cases, exercise books and spare stationery together before going to the shops.

Sort the supplies into three groups

Put the school list on the table. Make three groups: ready to take, already available at home, and still to buy. Let the children test their pens and check how many blank pages are left in the exercise books.

Write down the size and how many are missing

If you need three squared exercise books and already have two of the right size, add just one. You can put pens, books and glue on the EatMeFirst shopping list alongside your groceries. The inventory, shopping list and automatic browser saving are free, with no installation or sign-up.
