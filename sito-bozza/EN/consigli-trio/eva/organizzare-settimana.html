How to organise a family week around school and activities

Keep track of times, lifts and which bags need packing.

Write down who, when and who’s taking them

Go through the week’s plans together. For each activity, note who’s going, what time it starts and who’s taking them. Add a note if anything needs packing. For example, on Friday Gianni has judo at 5.30 pm and Maria has dance at 6 pm. Check both bags the night before and decide who’s taking each child.

Try it in the household agenda

Enter the two example activities before adding your own. Open the household agenda from the Menu. Inventory, shopping lists and automatic browser saving are free too. EatMeFirst opens with no installation or sign-up.
