How to organise a grocery list by aisle

Make a list that follows your route around the supermarket so you can find what you need.

Start with the meals you plan to cook

Write down two or three meals you expect to make. For each one, check the fridge and pantry, then add only what’s missing and how much. For courgette pasta, for example, write “3 courgettes” if you already have pasta and oil.

Group items in the order you shop

Put fruit and veg together, then pantry staples, chilled food and frozen items. If you switch supermarkets, arrange the groups in the order you usually pass those aisles. Tick each line as soon as it goes in your trolley. In EatMeFirst, you can make a shopping list with an empty pantry.
