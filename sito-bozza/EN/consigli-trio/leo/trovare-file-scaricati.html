Where to find downloaded files on iPhone and Android

Find downloads in your phone’s file app or browser and locate your EatMeFirst backup after exporting it.

On iPhone, check Files or Safari

Open Files, tap Browse, then look for Downloads. If you can’t see it, check iCloud Drive. The location can depend on your Safari settings. If you just downloaded an item in Safari, you can also tap the Downloads button near the search field.

On Android, check your file app or the Chrome menu

Open your file manager, such as Files, and look for Downloads. If you used Chrome, open the three-dot menu and tap Downloads. When you export an EatMeFirst backup, find it straight away and save a copy wherever you want to keep it. Inventory, shopping lists and browser saving are free, with no installation or sign-up.
