Tips from Leo

Files, backups and phone settings

Ask Leo a question

  • Camera not working in your browser? How to turn it back on

    Check Chrome permissions on Android or Safari settings on iPhone before scanning a barcode.

  • What does a food barcode tell your phone?

    Find out where ingredients and calories come from when you scan food packaging.

  • How to add a website to your phone’s home screen

    Open the site in the app you use to browse the web, such as Chrome, Edge or Safari.

  • Where to find downloads on your phone

    On iPhone, open Files and look for Downloads under Browse.

  • What it means to save data in your phone’s browser

    Your pantry normally stays in the same browser and profile on the same device.

  • How to name files so you can find the latest backup

    Use a consistent format such as pantry-2026-11-25-phone.

  • Product notes: ingredients and homemade preparations.

    A small note beside the product can save a lot of guessing later.

  • Broken phone or PC? Recover your pantry.

    This is when an external copy earns its place. Leo explains how to start again.
