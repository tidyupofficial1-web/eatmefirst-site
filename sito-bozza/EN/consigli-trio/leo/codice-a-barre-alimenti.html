What does a food barcode tell your phone?

Find out where ingredients and calories come from when you scan food packaging.

What is in an EAN barcode?

It identifies the product, such as a particular brand and size of yogurt. Matching cups carry the same barcode even when their date labels differ.

Where do ingredients and calories come from?

When you look up a food online in EatMeFirst, the app uses the code to search Open Food Facts. If the record includes ingredients or nutrition data, the app can display them. Check the package for missing details, especially allergens. Read the date on your own cup.
