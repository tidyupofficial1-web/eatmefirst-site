Camera not working in your browser? How to turn it back on

Check Chrome permissions on Android or Safari settings on iPhone before scanning a barcode.

On Android with Chrome

Open the site, tap the icon to the left of the address and look for Permissions. If camera access is blocked for that site, change the setting and try again. If Chrome itself lacks access, check Camera in the app permissions in Android Settings.

On iPhone with Safari

Open the site in Safari, tap the page menu and open Website Settings. Check the Camera setting for the site and try scanning again. In EatMeFirst, you can also type the digits under the barcode and run the online search.
