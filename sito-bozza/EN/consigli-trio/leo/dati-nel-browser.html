All tips from Leo

Where does your phone browser save your pantry data?

Find out where your pantry stays and what to check before switching browsers or phones.

Think of the browser as a notebook on your device

Open EatMeFirst at the same address with the same browser and profile and you’ll normally see your pantry again. Another browser, profile or phone uses a separate store, so the pantry there may look empty.

Check your backup before switching phones

Make sure you have a readable backup file you can reach from another device. If you’re freeing up space, check what you’re deleting: clearing site data can remove your pantry. Automatic browser saving is free. An external copy is an optional extra step explained in Utilities. EatMeFirst opens with no download or sign-up.
