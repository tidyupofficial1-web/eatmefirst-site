All tips from Max

How to organise vegetable and herb seed packets

Sort seed packets by sowing season, label opened ones and check what you have before buying more.

Group packets by sowing season

Put packets for the same sowing season together. Write the name on opened packets and follow the storage instructions on the packaging. Pick a dry spot where you can easily find them again.

Check your seed stash before buying

Look through the packets for the current season. If you already have two packets of basil, put only what’s missing on the list. Use EatMeFirst to record seeds and other garden supplies. The inventory, shopping list and automatic browser saving are free, with no download or sign-up.
