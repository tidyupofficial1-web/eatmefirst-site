How to repot supermarket basil

When to move it to a new pot, what potting mix to use and where to put it afterwards.

Check the roots first

Gently slide out the root ball. If roots are tightly circling the bottom, choose a slightly wider pot with drainage holes. Use potting mix and keep the base of the stems level with the soil as before. Water it and let the excess drain away.

Pick a bright, sheltered spot

Basil likes warmth and light. Keep it somewhere bright and sheltered from cold, and introduce direct sun gradually. Check the mix with a finger and water when the surface starts to dry. You can put the repotting date in the Household agenda and note in the Notepad where it grows best.
