How to keep a garden journal on your phone

Save planting dates, jobs and observations for the next growing season.

Keep calendar dates and jobs separate

Use the Household agenda for dates that matter, such as “Saturday: pot up the basil”. For jobs without a fixed date, open Shopping → To-do list. Add “buy potting mix” or “tie up the tomatoes” and tick each item off when it’s done.

Leave yourself a note for next season

Use the Notepad to record what you tried and what you noticed. One line is enough: “Basil on the east-facing balcony did better than basil in afternoon sun”. At the end of the season, read back through your notes and decide what to repeat or change. You can start with a text note and an empty EatMeFirst pantry.
