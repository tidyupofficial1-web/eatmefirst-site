Tips from Max

How to organise a bumper harvest from your vegetable garden

Count what you’ve picked and decide what to cook, give away or keep for upcoming meals.

Split your harvest into three groups

When lots of courgettes are ready at once, weigh them or count them. Keep today’s portion within reach and set aside the amount you want to give away. For the rest, decide when you’ll use it and how to store it based on what you’ve picked.

Check the harvest before you shop

Go through your harvest with your shopping list in hand. If you already have enough courgettes for two dinners, leave them off the list and buy only what you need to finish those meals.

Try it in EatMeFirst

Add the harvest manually, even if it has no barcode, and update it as you use it. The inventory, shopping list and automatic saving are free. EatMeFirst opens in your browser with no download or sign-up.
