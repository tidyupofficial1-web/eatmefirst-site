How to keep track of potting mix and garden supplies

Check pots, compost, plant ties and labels before heading to the garden centre.

Start with the jobs you’re planning

Choose your weekend jobs before writing the list. To pot up three seedlings, count your empty pots, check how much potting mix is left and see whether you already have labels. Keep opened bags at the front with their names easy to read.

Write down what runs out

If you use the last plant tie or empty a bag, add it to your list straight away. Give each tool a place where you can find it again. In EatMeFirst you can record potting mix and other supplies you use up. The inventory, list and browser saving are free, with no download or sign-up.
