How to organise jars in your pantry

Group jars by type, check the labels and bring the ones you want to use first to the front.

Group the jars and check their labels

Put ready-to-store jars into groups, such as jams and other jarred foods. Check that you can read what’s inside and the date you wrote down. If it helps, note when you opened the jar. Follow the product’s storage instructions.

Keep the jars you’ll use first within sight

When you put more jars away, leave a visible spot for the ones you plan to use first. Check that shelf before writing your shopping list. In EatMeFirst you can record the name, quantity and shelf. Inventory, shopping lists and automatic browser saving are free, with no installation or sign-up.
