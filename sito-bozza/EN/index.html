EATMEFIRST · LESS WASTE, MORE ORDER AT HOME

This is what should stop happening!

Something forgotten at the back of the fridge… and that smell when you open it. EatMeFirst helps you organise food and other household supplies before they slip your mind.

Let’s imagine a solution together ↓

EATMEFIRST

What would you want from a household inventory app?

Food, cleaning supplies, pet products and the other things you buy for your home. These are the wishes we started with. Are they yours too?

  • ✓

    No registration to get started.

    «I don’t want to give an email address, a Google login or contact details. Or invent another password.»

    You can start organising your pantry without creating an account.

  • ✓

    My habits stay on my own device.

    «I want my products, quantities and usage records to stay on my phone or PC.»

    Your pantry is stored in your device’s browser. Barcode lookup asks external catalogues about the code; it does not send them your shopping list or inventory.

  • ✓

    Plenty of features, explained simply.

    «I want to know what to do, even if I’m no expert.»

    The info buttons explain the different functions. Max, Eva and Leo lend a hand with simple words and a little humour.

  • ✓

    Products without a barcode too.

    «What about market vegetables, loose goods or something I made myself?»

    Type a name, quantity and location, with a date when appropriate. Include homemade jam, leftovers and meals you put in the freezer.

  • ✓

    Know what I have and where it is.

    «Pantry, fridge, garage… I don’t want to rely on memory.»

    Choose the locations and categories you need. Move just some items from a pack and see where they are.

  • ✓

    Record what is left.

    «Half a salami or bottle is still in the house!»

    Remove whole items or record the remaining content. Opening a product does not mean it should disappear from your inventory.

  • ✓

    Remember what to use first.

    «I want to notice approaching dates before finding something forgotten at the back of the fridge.»

    Inventory puts dates in order and alerts draw your attention. Strict dates and indicative dates are shown differently.

  • ✓

    A shopping list already off to a good start.

    «I want to find what has run out and add things as I think of them.»

    Recorded finished products go on the list. Add your own notes and tick items while shopping: they darken, are crossed out and move to the bottom.
