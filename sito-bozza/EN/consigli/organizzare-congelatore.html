Give each drawer a name and each homemade preparation a clear identity.

Lasagne for dinner… but where?

Name the locations clearly: ‘Freezer, top drawer’, ‘middle drawer’, ‘bottom drawer’. Use the same names consistently so everyone at home knows where to look.

Label the mystery package.

Write what it contains and when it was prepared and frozen on the container. ‘Lasagne, two portions’ is more useful than ‘leftovers’. In the app, enter it without a barcode, choose portions and location and add preparation details in the notes. Record usage when you take a portion.

Keep the physical and recorded locations together.

Filter Inventory by drawer before shopping. If you move a package, update its location too. Max, the freezer is not a treasure hunt! These are organisation tips: follow manufacturer instructions and official food-safety guidance for freezing, storage and thawing. The app does not decide food is safe.
