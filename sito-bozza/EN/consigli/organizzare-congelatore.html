All tips from Eva

How to organise freezer drawers and find your portions

Name the drawers, label your portions and find them in Inventory.

“There’s a portion of lasagne… but where?”

“If I have to shift half the freezer to find dinner, something needs sorting out. I’d start by giving everything a place.”

Give each drawer a name

Use simple names, such as top, middle and bottom drawer. Pick names that everyone at home will recognise.

Write the contents and dates on each container

Write down what’s inside and when you prepared and froze it. “Lasagne, two portions” tells you more than “leftovers”. The label stays useful when your phone isn’t to hand.

Use the same system in the app

Add a homemade dish by name even if it has no barcode. Enter the portions and storage location and note the preparation and freezing dates. When you take a portion, record it in the consumption screen. Before shopping, filter Inventory by drawer. If you move a container, update its location in the app too.

“And if Max moves everything around, he could at least tell me. The freezer isn’t a treasure hunt!”

For advice on freezing, storage time and defrosting, follow the producer’s instructions and official food safety guidance. The app helps you organise your freezer. Check food safety separately.
