All tips from Max

How to avoid buying groceries you already have

Check every storage spot, what’s left in opened packs and what’s on your shopping list before you leave.

Check all locations and how much is left

Search for the product in Inventory and select All locations. Pasta that’s missing from the kitchen might be in the cellar. A full pack and one at 50% contain different amounts. Check the notes too: a pack could be set aside for an occasion.

Use the list and keep it current

Write what you need and add a quantity or brand in the same entry if useful. Green suggestions flag items you still have but might run out of. Decide whether to buy more: before a holiday you might want to use up your stock. If you made the list on a computer, transfer the current copy to your phone. The two inventories don’t merge automatically, so avoid updating them separately. When you get home, record your purchases in Add products. Ticking items at the shop updates the list, while adding the purchase updates your pantry.
