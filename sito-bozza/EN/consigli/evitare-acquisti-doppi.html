One small habit before going out can keep the cupboards from overflowing.

Is the pasta finished, or in the other cupboard?

Search Inventory by name and select all locations, including the cellar stock. Check partial contents and reserved notes: a half packet is not a full one, and a package might be intended for an event.

Suggestions help; you decide.

Write quantities and brands directly in shopping notes. Green suggestions concern products still at home that might not last. If you are going away, you may prefer to finish what is there.

Before leaving and after returning.

If you also use a PC, transfer the updated situation to the phone first. Do not edit both copies separately expecting a merger. After shopping, record the actual purchase through Add products. A tick on the shopping list does not add it to the pantry, even if you chose a different brand.
