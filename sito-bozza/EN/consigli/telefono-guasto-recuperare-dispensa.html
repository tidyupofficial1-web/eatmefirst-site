Broken phone or PC? Recover your pantry.

This is when an external copy earns its place. Leo explains how to start again.

Leo

This is when an external copy earns its place. Leo explains how to start again.

Find the copy kept elsewhere.

Look for an Export pantry file whose name begins ‘EatMeFirst-dispensa’ and ends ‘.json’. It may be on a PC, USB drive or an email you sent yourself. Another folder on the same broken phone is not another device.

1. Transfer the file and open EatMeFirst.

Copy the file onto the new phone or PC. Do not edit its contents. Open the app from the official website. On a new device the introduction and conditions may appear again: read them and tick acceptance if you agree, then reach the menu.

2. Utilities → Import pantry.

Choose the file, check the summary and confirm only the situation you want. Import replaces everything; it does not merge two pantries. If the copy contains 100 products and you have entered only today’s milk on the new phone, write that new entry down, restore the 100 products and enter the milk again. If exporting the small new situation, keep it under a separate filename without overwriting the larger copy. Importing the milk-only file afterwards would replace the other products again. Choose by contents, not merely the newest date or highest count.

3. Check Inventory and shopping.

Recover what was included in the copy, then re-enter changes made afterwards. Dates do not start again from today. Catalogue pictures may need internet. An older useful copy is better than nothing, provided you correct it against the stock you really have.

No copy available?

Check another device you used. If the old device cannot be recovered and no external copy exists, you must enter products again: we have no server copy to return. Ask a technician before resetting or erasing a faulty device if you want to try recovery.

For next time, keep a spare key outside the house.

Automatic browser saving is useful on the current device. After important updates, also export and keep the file elsewhere. If using two devices, transfer the updated situation before working on the other; separate changes are not automatically combined.
