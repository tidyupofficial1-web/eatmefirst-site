THE SHOPPING LIST · WITH EVA

Before we go out, let’s see what’s missing.

Eva

“Here I am! These are the things we women tend to take care of… at least in my house: I send Max shopping, but I write the list! Of course, anyone can do it. What matters is not coming home with three jars of something we already have and forgetting the milk.”

One list, three kinds of reminder.

Here you’ll find products you’ve run out of, suggestions for things that might not last, and anything you add yourself. The colours help you tell them apart.

Red: it’s all gone.

When you record in Consumption that a product has run out, it goes onto the shopping list. You don’t have to remember to write it down again.

Green: it might not last.

You still have some, but the app estimates it could run out before your next shopping trip. It shows how much you have; you decide whether to buy more.

Grey: you added it.

Something new, a treat or an occasional purchase: write down whatever you need, even if it has never been in your inventory.

To make a prediction, we need to get to know each other first.

Green suggestions need at least two months of recorded purchases and usage. The app uses the available data to estimate how long your stock lasts and when you might next go shopping. They are suggestions, not certainties: the more regularly you record usage, the more useful they can become.

Write as you would on a scrap of paper.

In the field for adding a product, you can write, for example, ‘2 jars of jam, favourite brand’ or ‘batteries for the remote’. Press Add and it appears on the list.

There’s no need for a separate quantity box: if a quantity helps, just write it in the note.

At the supermarket, tick it and go.

When you put a product in your trolley, tap its little square. The entry becomes darker, its text is crossed out and it moves to the bottom, leaving the things you still need at the top. If you ticked it by mistake, untick it to put it back among the things to buy.

Ticking updates the list; it does not add the product to your inventory. When you get home, record the purchase in Add products, with the quantities and dates for the new product.

The suggestion is there. You have the final say.

“If I’m going on holiday and want to empty the fridge, I don’t buy everything again just because the app suggests it! I can tick an entry to put it aside, or delete it with the X. If the product later runs out and I record that in Consumption, it appears again as finished.”

Eva

“If you already use your phone, your list is right there with you. If you prepared it on your PC, remember to transfer your updated inventory to your phone before going out. The two devices don’t update each other automatically… and Max still hasn’t learnt to read minds!”
