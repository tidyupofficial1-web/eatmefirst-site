Optional analytics and privacy

Additional notice · 16 November 2026

EatMeFirst uses Google Analytics 4 only after you select Accept, to understand visits, pages, internal navigation, demos and videos and improve the site and app. Reject or Close lets you continue without enabling it. No Analytics tag or signal is sent to Google before consent.

After consent Google receives cookie identifiers, browser and device technical information, language, page URLs without query strings or fragments, and navigation and video events. Google also receives your IP address during connection; Google states that GA4 does not log or store IP addresses. We do not send pantry contents, products, quantities, expiry dates, photos, form contents, user emails or device names. We do not track email opens.

These statistics are not used for personalised advertising: Google Signals and ad personalisation are disabled in our code, and advertising consent remains denied. Enhanced measurement is disabled for this stream. The _ga and _ga_* cookies are configured to expire after at most 180 days without automatic renewal by our configuration. Your choice is stored locally in your browser and is separate from accepting the app terms.

Use Analytics privacy on measured pages, then Withdraw consent, to stop tracking, remove Analytics cookies accessible to the site and reload the page. This applies to this browser; other devices have separate choices. Withdrawal does not retroactively delete data already sent. The EatMeFirst property currently retains event data for 2 months and user data for 14 months; these settings do not limit most aggregated reports. New activity may restart the user-data period under Google settings.

Service: Google Analytics, provided by Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC; processing may involve the United States. See Google information below for recipients, transfers and applicable safeguards. For EatMeFirst data questions or requests contact eatmefirst.contatti@outlook.com. You may request information, access or erasure where applicable and contact your competent supervisory authority. Do not send your pantry to exercise these rights.
