How much did you use? Let’s see what is left.

Remove whole items, record partial use, move stock or add a reminder.

Leo

‘Max and Eva asked me to explain this one, since I built it. No computer jargon: just tell the app what you took or used!’

Used a whole item?

Choose Remove items, enter how many eggs or bottles you used and check the summary: available, to remove and remaining. Confirm to update the pantry immediately.

Only used part of it?

Choose Partial use and set how much remains. Half a milk carton or loaf means 50% remaining. If you use a quarter of a full product, 75% remains. The slider can show 22%, 31%, 78% or another percentage: it is not limited to halves and quarters.

Moved it rather than used it?

Use Move items for eligible products: choose the number and destination. Moving two bottles from the pantry to the fridge changes their location, not the total in your home.

Notes and reminders.

Write ‘Reserved for Sunday’ or ‘For Mum’. You can enter an event date and how many days beforehand you want a reminder. The app warns if the product date is before the event. Recent available actions can be undone from Last 15 operations; later actions on the same product may have to be undone first.

Saved locally, straight away.

A successfully confirmed operation is saved automatically in this browser. You do not need a second save. Use Export pantry separately to keep a complete copy outside the device. When a product runs out after a recorded removal, it can appear on the shopping list.
