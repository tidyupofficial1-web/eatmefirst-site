A New Year gift for our members: a more organised home and less waste.

EatMeFirst is an online app for organising food supplies and household products, keeping track of expiry dates, recording use and preparing a shopping list.

These and other useful features are free to use, with no time limit. Use the app online on your phone, without registering or installing a program.

Changes to your inventory are saved automatically and immediately on the phone you use, free of charge. Your product and usage records are not sent to our servers.

A short learning game introduces the features using sample products.

The link for members leads to the EatMeFirst website. There they can meet Max, Eva and Leo, explore the features, try the game and then open the app.
