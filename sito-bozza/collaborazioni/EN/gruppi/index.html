EatMeFirst is an online app for organising food supplies and household products, keeping track of expiry dates, recording use and preparing a shopping list.

We are preparing the international launch of EatMeFirst and want to introduce it to as many people as possible. We are reaching out to groups and associations that care about saving money and reducing waste. Your recommendation can help us reach interested people and gather suggestions to improve the app.

These and other useful features are free to use, with no time limit. Use the app online on your phone, without registering or installing a program.

Changes to your inventory are saved automatically and immediately on the phone you use, free of charge.

Your product and usage records are not sent to our servers.

A short learning game introduces the features using sample products.

We do not ask for purchases, fees, your members’ details or advertising in return. You can share the announcement on your website, noticeboard or newsletter, or display a poster at your collection point. If you print it, only paper and printing are needed.

Choose colour or black and white, with or without a space for your group’s name, logo or stamp. Personalising the poster is optional.

Start on your phone

EatMeFirst is easy to read on a phone screen. Your camera can scan product barcodes while you put your shopping away. It also works on a PC, where you can enter products manually, even without a barcode.

The link for members leads to the EatMeFirst website. There they can meet Max, Eva and Leo, explore the features, try the game and then open the app.
