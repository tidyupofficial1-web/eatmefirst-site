# EatMeFirst — Official Website

Welcome to the official repository for the **EatMeFirst** website and public landing page.

---

## 🇬🇧 English

EatMeFirst is a privacy-first home pantry management application designed around how people actually cook, shop, and live.

### Key Features
* **Real-World Inventory & Locations:** Track where products are stored (fridge, freezer, pantry) and keep your kitchen organized.
* **No Barcode? No Problem:** Easily handle fresh market items, artisanal products, or local meat without standard barcodes.
* **Smart Stock & Expiry Tracking:** Monitor item availability and receive alerts for upcoming expiration dates to eliminate food waste.
* **100% Local-First & Private:** Zero server-side tracking and zero cloud dependency. All data stays securely on your device, with optional real-time backup to a personal USB drive.
* **Multilingual Experience:** Fully localized for English, Italian, Spanish, French, and German.
