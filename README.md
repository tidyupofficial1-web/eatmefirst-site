# EatMeFirst — Official Website

Welcome to the official repository for the **EatMeFirst** website and public landing page.

EatMeFirst is not just another basic expiration tracker. It is a comprehensive, privacy-first home pantry management system designed around how people *actually* cook, shop, and live.

## 🚀 What Makes EatMeFirst Different?
- **Real-World Inventory & Locations:** It’s not just a flat list of dates. EatMeFirst tracks *where* things are stored in your home (fridge, freezer, pantry) and how spaces are organized.
- **No Barcode? No Problem:** Seamlessly handle fresh market items, artisanal cheeses, local produce, or butcher meat that don't come with standard barcodes.
- **Decimals Consumption (Consumo a Taglio):** Don't just deduct whole packages. Use our precise decimal system (1/10, 2/10, 5/10...) to track partial usage—like using half a pack of flour or a splash of milk—keeping your inventory accurate to the millimeter.
- **100% Local-First & Private:** Zero server-side tracking, zero cloud dependency. All your data stays securely on your device, backed up instantly to your personal USB flash drive.
- **Multilingual Experience:** Fully localized for Italian, English, French, Spanish, and German.
